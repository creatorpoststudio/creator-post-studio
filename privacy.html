Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Creator Post Studio accesses, why, how it is stored, and how you can have it deleted. It applies to creators who connect a TikTok account to Creator Post Studio.

Creator Post Studio — Privacy Policy  |  Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Who this policy covers

Creator Post Studio (“we”, “the tool”) is a publishing tool that helps creators post their own videos to their own TikTok accounts using TikTok’s official Content Posting API. This policy covers anyone who connects a TikTok account to Creator Post Studio or uses it to upload and post a video.

2. Data we access

We access only the data needed to connect your account and post the videos you choose:

  • Basic TikTok account information — your username, display name, and profile picture, provided by TikTok under the user.info.basic permission. This is used to identify and display the connected account.
  • Access tokens — when you authorize the connection on TikTok, TikTok issues an access token (and, where provided, a refresh token) for your account. This token is what allows the tool to post on your behalf.
  • Videos you choose to upload — the video files, captions, and posting settings you provide in the tool for the purpose of posting them to your TikTok account, under the video.upload and video.publish permissions.

We do not receive or store your TikTok password. Sign-in and authorization happen on TikTok’s own pages. We do not access your direct messages, contacts, or private account content.

3. How we use your data

Your data is used for one purpose: operating the posting tool for you. Specifically:

  • To show which TikTok account is connected, so posts go to the right account.
  • To upload and publish the specific videos you selected — either as a direct post or as a draft/inbox upload, according to your choice for each video.
  • To keep your account connected between sessions, so you do not have to re-authorize before every post.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to posting the videos you selected.

4. How access tokens are stored

Access tokens are stored securely and are treated as credentials. They are used only to post the videos you selected to your own TikTok account through TikTok’s official API. A token is never used to access an account it was not issued for, and it stops working as soon as you revoke the app’s access in TikTok or disconnect your account in the tool.

5. Sharing — we do not sell or share your data

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing, advertising, analytics resale, or any other purpose. The only transfer that takes place is the one required to do what you asked: your selected video and its posting details are sent to TikTok through TikTok’s official Content Posting API so it can be posted to your account. TikTok’s handling of content posted to its platform is governed by TikTok’s own terms and privacy policy.

6. Data retention

We keep the basic account information and access token for a connected account only for as long as that account remains connected, so the tool can post for you. When you disconnect your account, revoke access in TikTok, or ask us to delete your data, the stored information associated with your account is deleted as described in Section 7.

7. Your control and data deletion

You are in control of the connection at all times:

  • Disconnect at any time. Revoke Creator Post Studio in TikTok under Settings → Manage app permissions, or disconnect your account in the tool. This immediately invalidates the access token.
  • Request full deletion. Any creator may request deletion of the data stored for their account. On a deletion request — or on disconnection — the basic account information and tokens stored for that account are removed. See Data deletion for the exact steps.
  • Choose what gets posted. No video is posted unless you uploaded it and selected how it should be posted.
Deleting a post on TikTok: removing stored connection data does not remove videos already published to your TikTok profile. Published videos remain under your control in TikTok and can be deleted there at any time, like any other TikTok post.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored access tokens and account information against unauthorized access. No system is perfectly secure, but tokens are handled as credentials, access to them is limited to what the posting function requires, and they are never displayed publicly or shared.

9. Children

Creator Post Studio is intended for creators who are permitted to hold a TikTok account under TikTok’s terms. It is not directed at children below the minimum age required by TikTok in their country.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes to what data is accessed or how it is used will be reflected here before they take effect.

11. Contact and deletion requests

For any privacy question, or to request deletion of the data stored for your TikTok account, contact the operator of Creator Post Studio through the channel where you received access to the tool, identifying the TikTok username of the connected account. Deletion requests are actioned for that account’s stored basic information and access tokens.

Data deletion

Two ways, either one works. Disconnecting stops all future posting immediately; deletion removes the stored information for your account.

Option A — Revoke in TikTok

Open TikTok and go to your profile. Open Settings and privacy → Manage app permissions (Security & permissions on some versions). Select Creator Post Studio and remove its access. Your access token is invalidated at once, and the stored data associated with your account is removed.

Option B — Request deletion

Contact the operator of Creator Post Studio through the channel where you received access to the tool and state the TikTok username of the account you connected. We will delete the basic account information and access tokens stored for that account.

Videos already published to TikTok are not affected by connection-data deletion — they stay on your profile under your control and can be deleted in TikTok at any time.
