What is Creator Post Studio?

Creator Post Studio is a tool built for creators who publish on TikTok regularly and want a simpler, more controlled way to get finished videos onto their own accounts.

What it does

Once a creator connects their TikTok account, they can upload a finished video in Creator Post Studio and send it to that same account through TikTok’s official Content Posting API — either published directly, or delivered as a draft/inbox upload so the creator can review and post it inside TikTok.

  • Creator-selected videos only. The tool posts what the creator uploads and confirms — nothing else.
  • One app, each account separate. Every creator authorizes their own account and gets their own access token.
  • Built on TikTok’s official API. No scraping, no password sharing, no unofficial automation.

What it is not

Creator Post Studio is an independent tool. It is not TikTok, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or ByteDance. It does not buy, sell, or trade engagement, and it never posts content a creator did not choose.

  • It never asks for your TikTok password.
  • It does not read your messages, contacts, or private content.
  • It does not post to any account that has not connected and consented.

From finished video to TikTok in three steps

The creator stays in control at every step. Connecting an account takes a single authorization on TikTok, and every post starts with a video the creator deliberately uploaded.

Step 1 — Connect your TikTok account

Tap connect and you are taken to TikTok’s official authorization screen. Sign in on TikTok itself, review the permissions, and approve. TikTok then issues Creator Post Studio an access token for your account.

You can revoke this at any time in TikTok under Settings → Manage app permissions. Revoking immediately stops any further posting.

Step 2 — Upload your video

Add the finished video you want to publish and set its caption and posting options. Only videos you upload here are ever sent to TikTok — the tool has no access to your device, camera roll, or existing TikTok library beyond what you provide.

Nothing is sent yet. Uploading prepares the post; it does not publish anything on its own.

Step 3 — Post directly, or save as a draft

Choose Direct Post to publish the video to your TikTok profile through the Content Posting API, or choose the draft / inbox option to receive the video in TikTok, where you can review it and post it yourself from the app.

Your choice, per video. Each upload is posted only in the way you selected for that video.

Exactly what the app can access

Creator Post Studio requests only the TikTok permissions it needs to identify your account and post your videos. These are the scopes shown on TikTok’s consent screen when you connect.

user.info.basic — Basic account information

Your TikTok username, display name, and profile picture — used only to show you which account is connected, so a video is never posted to the wrong account.

video.upload — Upload a video as a draft

Lets the tool send a video you selected to your TikTok account as an unpublished draft/inbox item, which you then review and post yourself inside TikTok.

video.publish — Publish a video directly

Lets the tool publish a video you selected directly to your TikTok profile, when you choose the direct post option for that video.

That is the full list. Creator Post Studio does not request access to your password, direct messages, contacts, followers list, or payment information, and it cannot post anything after you disconnect your account.

How to disconnect and delete your data

Two ways, either one works. Disconnecting stops all future posting immediately; deletion removes the stored information for your account.

Option A — Revoke in TikTok

The fastest way, entirely inside TikTok:

  1. Open TikTok and go to your profile.
  2. Open Settings and privacy → Manage app permissions (Security & permissions on some versions).
  3. Select Creator Post Studio and remove its access.

Your access token is invalidated at once, and the stored data associated with your account is removed.

Option B — Request deletion

Contact the operator of Creator Post Studio through the channel where you received access to the tool and state the TikTok username of the account you connected. We will delete the basic account information and access tokens stored for that account.

Videos already published to TikTok are not affected by connection-data deletion — they stay on your profile under your control and can be deleted in TikTok at any time.

Common questions

Does Creator Post Studio need my TikTok password?

No. You sign in and approve the connection on TikTok’s own authorization screen. TikTok gives the tool an access token — your password is never seen, asked for, or stored by Creator Post Studio.

Can it post videos I didn’t choose?

No. The tool can only send videos that you uploaded into it and confirmed, using the posting option you selected for that video — direct post or draft. It has no access to your camera roll or your existing TikTok videos.

What is the difference between direct post and draft?

A direct post is published to your TikTok profile through the Content Posting API. A draft (inbox upload) delivers the video to your TikTok account unpublished, so you can review it, make final edits, and post it yourself inside TikTok.

Can one app connect more than one TikTok account?

Yes. Each TikTok account authorizes separately on TikTok’s consent screen and receives its own access token. A video is only ever posted to the account it was prepared for.

How do I stop it from posting to my account?

Revoke Creator Post Studio under TikTok’s Manage app permissions, or disconnect in the tool. The access token stops working immediately, and no further posts can be made. See data deletion for full removal of stored data.
